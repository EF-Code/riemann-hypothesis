# Review of the final reduction

The archive's `riemannHypothesis` uses Mathlib's definition. The proof term calls `Formalism.VW.rh_of_every` with `allChi`, the archive's claim that Dirichlet L-functions have no zeros to the right of the line `Re(s) = 1/2`, with the principal character at `s = 1` excepted. The auxiliary check preserves the source proof bodies for the reduction and supplies this nonvanishing claim as an explicit hypothesis. [MainTheorems.lean](../upstream/extracted/lean/src/Supplements/Kernels/MainTheorems.lean)

The RH reduction takes the character modulo 1 to recover the Riemann zeta function. For a putative nontrivial zero to the left of the line, the functional equation at `1 - s` relates that zero to the product of a nonzero power of `2π`, a gamma factor, a cosine factor, and `ζ(1 - s)`. The gamma factor is nonzero in that half-plane. Vanishing of the cosine factor would put `s` at an even integer. Its position, the value at zero, and exclusion of the negative even integers rule this out. The hypothesis supplies the remaining nonzero factor. [CritLine.lean](../upstream/extracted/lean/src/Formalism/GammaFactorNonvanishing/CritLine.lean) · [RhOfRight.lean](../upstream/extracted/lean/src/Formalism/GammaFactorNonvanishing/RhOfRight.lean)

For an imprimitive character, the reduction first removes the extra Euler factors. In `0 < Re(s) < 1`, each factor `1 - χ(p) p^(-s)` is nonzero because `|χ(p) p^(-s)| < 1`. For a primitive character, the functional equation reflects a zero to the inverse character at `1 - s`, where the same hypothesis applies. This yields the Dirichlet critical-strip statement. [ClaimStrip.lean](../upstream/extracted/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ClaimStrip.lean)

The unconditional step is the archive's construction of `allChi`. Its entry point is `UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter`, which invokes an automorphic and Galois-theoretic argument. The statements and final implication can be reviewed locally while this larger proof builds. [MainConclusion.lean](../upstream/extracted/lean/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean)
